The Meenakshi Amman Temple stands in the historic city of Madurai and is dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi and Lord Sundareswarar.

The temple tradition has ancient associations, while the monumental complex visible today developed through successive periods of construction, expansion and patronage.

Centuries in Motion
