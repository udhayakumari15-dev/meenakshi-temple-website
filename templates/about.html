The Sacred Centre

A Temple
Like No Other

Located in the historic city of Madurai, the Meenakshi Amman Temple is dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi and Lord Sundareswarar.

The temple complex is renowned for its Dravidian architectural character, monumental gopurams, sculptural richness and sacred spaces. It continues to function not simply as a historic monument, but as a living place of worship.
